Known units: g, kg, ml, cl, dl, l, tsp, tbsp, cup, oz, lb, pcs, can, pack (also stk, spsk, tsk). Add your own here, separated by commas.

The item on the left is treated as the item on the right, so both merge on the shopping list. Already built in: scallion, cilantro, zucchini, ground beef, red/yellow/white onion and more. Write “red onion = red onion” to keep one of them separate.

“As written” keeps dl, cl, ounces and cups when your recipes use them. Metric and imperial convert and merge everything.
